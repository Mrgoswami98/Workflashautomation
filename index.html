FAQ

Questions clients ask us

We are a small business. Is automation worth it for us?

Yes. Small teams often benefit the most, because every hour saved goes straight to sales and customers. We start with low-cost Level 1–2 automations that pay back quickly.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build, set up and train your team. You keep using familiar tools like WhatsApp, Excel, Google Sheets or a simple dashboard.

Why Python and Anaconda?

Python is the world's leading language for data and AI. Anaconda gives us stable, reproducible environments, so the analytics, reports and AI models we build run reliably on your systems.

How long does a project take?

Simple automations go live in 3–7 days. Multi-system projects typically take 2–6 weeks, and enterprise roll-outs are phased.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We work in your own accounts wherever possible, use access controls, and never share or resell your data.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on scope and complexity. After a free audit you get a fixed quote along with the expected savings, with no hidden costs.
