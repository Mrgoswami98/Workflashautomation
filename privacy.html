Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Your trust matters to us. Here is, in plain language, how Workflash Automation handles personal data collected through this website.

Who we are

This website, workflash.in, is run by Workflash Automation ("we", "us"), a business automation service provider in India founded by Ankush Goswami. This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: when you use the quote form, WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive your name, mobile number and, if you provide them, your email, company name, company size, timeline, the services you are interested in and your message.
  • Calculator and quiz answers: the ROI calculator and Readiness Score run in your browser. Their results are only sent to us if you choose to add them to your enquiry.
  • Technical data: like most websites, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser and pages visited. If we enable Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel, they use cookies to measure visits and ad performance.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare an automation plan and quote.
  • To contact you about your enquiry or project by phone, WhatsApp or email.
  • To understand how visitors use the website and improve it.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements when you become a client.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

Consent

By sending an enquiry through the form, WhatsApp, phone or email, you consent to us using your details for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already done.

Services that handle your data

We use trusted third-party services to run this website and respond to you. They process data under their own privacy policies:

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting
  • WhatsApp (Meta): messages you send us
  • Google (Gmail, Google Sheets, Google Analytics): email, enquiry records and website statistics
  • Meta Pixel: advertising measurement, only if enabled
  • Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude: AI replies in the Flash 5.0 chat
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: fonts and the Python Lab runtime loaded in your browser

Flash 5.0 chat assistant

Our website chat assistant, Flash 5.0, helps you describe your business problem and suggests a solution.

  • Messages you type in the chat are sent to our Google Apps Script backend and to an AI provider (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) to generate replies. If AI is switched off, replies come from a built-in script in your browser.
  • Chat messages and replies are saved in our Google Sheet so our team can follow up and improve answers. If you share your name or phone number in the chat, we may contact you about your enquiry.
  • Please do not share passwords, OTPs, bank or card details in the chat.
  • The chat history in your browser is kept only for the current browser tab session.

Cookies

The website itself does not need cookies to work. Analytics and advertising tools, when enabled, set cookies to count visits and measure campaigns. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the website will still work.

How long we keep data

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and follow up, and delete enquiry data we no longer need, generally within 24 months of our last interaction. Client records are kept as long as required by law.

Client project data

When we build automation for a client, we work in the client's own accounts wherever possible, access only the data the project needs, and handle it as agreed in the project agreement. We never share or resell client data.

Your rights

Under Indian data protection law you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop using it by withdrawing your consent;
  • address a grievance about how we handled it.

Write to us using the contact details below. We will reply within a reasonable time, and within any time limit set by law.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as access controls and secure, reputable service providers. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
