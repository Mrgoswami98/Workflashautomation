About Workflash

Our Journey

  1. 2019The problem
  2. 2021Workflash founded
  3. TodaySame vision

In 2019, while working in the private sector, Ankush closely observed a common challenge faced by many small and growing businesses. Across SMEs, MSMEs, and small-scale enterprises, countless businesses were operating without proper systems, structured processes, or automation.

Business owners were spending a significant part of their day following up with employees, managing repetitive tasks, checking reports, solving operational issues, and putting out fires. Instead of focusing on growth, strategy, customers, and expansion, they were constantly caught up in day-to-day firefighting.

Ankush believed that businesses should not have to choose between managing operations and growing their business.

With a vision to solve this problem, in 2021, Ankush founded Workflash Automation.

The idea was simple but powerful: bring systems, structure, technology, and automation to businesses that are still dependent on manual processes.

Workflash Automation was built with a mission to help SMEs, MSMEs, startups, and small-scale businesses transform the way they operate by replacing repetitive manual work with smart, reliable, and scalable systems.

From business process automation and CRM solutions to dashboards, reporting systems, workflow automation, AI-powered solutions, and custom software, our goal is to build technology around the way a business actually works.

We don't believe in automation just for the sake of technology. We automate to create freedom.

  • Freedom for business owners from unnecessary follow-ups.
  • Freedom from scattered data and manual reporting.
  • Freedom from repetitive operational tasks.
  • And most importantly, freedom to focus on what truly matters — growing the business.

Today, Workflash Automation continues to follow the same vision that started in 2021:

“Don't spend your time fires fighting. Build systems that prevent them.”

Our mission is to help businesses move from

  1. Manual
  2. Systematic
  3. Automated
  4. Intelligent

so they can spend less time managing chaos and more time building the future.

FAQ

Questions clients ask us

We are a small business. Is automation worth it for us?

Yes. Small teams often benefit the most, because every hour saved goes straight to sales and customers. We start with low-cost Level 1–2 automations that pay back quickly.

Kya aap Hindi mein baat karte hain?

Haan. Meetings, training aur WhatsApp support Hindi ya English mein, jo aapko aasaan lage. Hum AI chatbots bhi Hindi aur English dono mein bana sakte hain.

Do we need technical staff?

No. We build, set up and train your team. You keep using familiar tools like WhatsApp, Excel, Google Sheets or a simple dashboard.

Why Python and Anaconda?

Python is the world's leading language for data and AI. Anaconda gives us stable, reproducible environments, so the analytics, reports and AI models we build run reliably on your systems.

How long does a project take?

Simple automations go live in 3–7 days. Multi-system projects typically take 2–6 weeks, and enterprise roll-outs are phased.

Is our data safe?

Yes. We work in your own accounts wherever possible, use access controls, and never share or resell your data.

How much does automation cost?

Basic automations start from ₹15,000 – ₹40,000, systems for growing SMEs (CRM, delegation, attendance, inventory, dashboards) are usually ₹50,000 – ₹1,50,000, and advanced AI or multi-department projects start around ₹1,50,000. These are market-based ranges, exclusive of GST. After a free audit you get a fixed quote along with the expected savings, with no hidden costs.
