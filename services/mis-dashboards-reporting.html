FAQ

Common questions

Which tool is best: Excel, Google Sheets or Power BI?

It depends on your team. Google Sheets is easy to share, Excel suits offline work, and Power BI is best for larger data and interactive dashboards. We often start simple and scale up.

Can you use our existing Excel reports?

Yes. We usually start from your current reports, keep the format your team knows and automate the manual steps behind them.

How often can the data refresh?

From once a day to every few minutes, depending on the source and tool.

Is our data safe?

We work in your own Google, Microsoft or server accounts wherever possible and give access only to the people who need it.
