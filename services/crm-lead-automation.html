FAQ

Common questions

Can you connect IndiaMART and JustDial leads?

Yes. Depending on your plan with the portal, we use its API, lead emails or exports to bring leads into your CRM or Google Sheet automatically.

We don't have a CRM. What do you suggest?

For small teams a well-built Google Sheets CRM is often enough to start. Growing teams usually move to Zoho or HubSpot. We recommend based on team size and budget.

Will my salespeople need training?

Yes, a short one. We train the team and share simple guides, and the system is built around how they already work.

Can I see salesperson-wise performance?

Yes. We build reports for leads received, response time, qualification and conversion per salesperson and per source.
